# Player Card Generation Rules

## Pose Rule

- Do not use pose images as loose inspiration.
- Pick exactly one source pose image for each generated player.
- Imitate the selected source pose closely:
  - torso lean
  - shoulder angle
  - elbow bend
  - racket height
  - racket face direction
  - wrist angle
  - non-racket arm position
  - leg stance and weight shift
- Do not blend multiple pose references into an averaged pose.
- Use montage sheets only as a catalog for choosing a pose, not as the main generation reference.

## Hand Scale Rule

- Hands must match the 3-head character body's scale.
- The racket hand may be slightly emphasized for readability, but not oversized.
- Fingers must have adult proportions and natural thickness.
- No missing thumb or index finger.
- No extra fingers, melted fingers, or childlike tiny hands.
- Racket handle size must match the hand size.

## Shakehand Grip Rule

- Use a real shakehand grip, not penholder.
- The handle runs diagonally through the palm.
- Middle, ring, and little fingers wrap around the handle.
- The thumb rests near the blade shoulder on the forehand/front side.
- The index finger lies along the backhand/back side near the lower blade and handle junction.
- Do not draw a clenched fist hiding the handle.
- Do not place the index finger across the red front face.
- Do not use a V pinch at the blade neck.

## Penholder Grip Rule

- Use a real traditional penholder grip, not shakehand.
- The hand sits high at the blade neck.
- Thumb and index finger visibly pinch/clamp the short handle and blade neck from the front/top side.
- Thumb and index should form a clear V around the handle/blade shoulder.
- Middle, ring, and little fingers support the back side of the blade or handle.
- The handle protrudes only a short distance downward.
- Do not wrap the whole hand around the handle like shakehand.

## Style Rule

- Use the approved v5/v6 card-character style: polished 3D arcade sports-game caricature, roughly 3-head adult proportions.
- Keep body proportions consistent across all players.
- Faces should be simplified but recognizable by strong features from the source photo.
- Uniforms should be real table-tennis round-neck athletic shirts, not plain black shirts and not polo collars.
- Full shoes must be visible and solid.
